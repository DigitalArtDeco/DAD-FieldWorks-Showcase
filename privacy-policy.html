General information

The company website of DigitalArtDeco Labs UG (haftungsbeschränkt) is temporarily paused. The domain www.dadlabs.de automatically redirects to the public showcase repository on github.com. German and English legal information remains available on this domain. This policy describes processing when the redirect and these legal pages are requested.

Hosting through GitHub Pages

The public website at www.dadlabs.de is hosted through GitHub Pages. According to GitHub, a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes when a website hosted through GitHub Pages is visited. Technical access data necessary to deliver and secure the website, prevent abuse or analyse errors may also be processed. These may include information about the request, device and usage.

Processing serves to provide this website securely, reliably, without technical errors and economically, and to prevent abuse. For DigitalArtDeco Labs UG, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the secure, reliable and economical provision of this website.

GitHub and its technical service providers or subprocessors may receive technical data. It has not been established for this website that DigitalArtDeco Labs UG generally has access to all logs maintained by GitHub. Further information is available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The basis used in an individual case depends on the specific transfer. Further details are provided in the current GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Redirect to GitHub

When you visit the homepage or a previous showcase page, your browser first loads the redirect page from GitHub Pages. It then automatically opens the public showcase repository on github.com. The redirect does not forward URL parameters or fragments to its destination. The page uses the no-referrer referrer policy.

The first request already involves the GitHub Pages hosting processing described above. The redirect does not prevent it. During the subsequent visit to github.com, GitHub processes data under its own privacy information and cookie information. Any cookie controls offered there are provided by GitHub and are not controlled by this redirect page.

Cookies and technical implementation during the pause

The redirect and the legal pages on this domain set no new persistent cookies and use no analytics or advertising trackers. They do not embed external font files, images, videos, maps or social plugins. There is no contact form. Styling is included directly in the HTML files and fonts come from the visitor’s device.

The previously offered optional cookie __Host-dadlabs_notice is no longer used. When one of these pages is opened, a short script attempts to delete only that cookie on the current host using an expired cookie assignment. It stores no new preference and reads no cookie contents. Without JavaScript, a previously saved cookie may remain until its original expiry and may continue to be sent to the host by the browser. You may delete it at any time using your browser’s website data settings.

Cookie consent is no longer requested. Processing to deliver and secure the redirect and legal pages remains independent of this change. Information about IP logging is available in the GitHub Pages documentation.

Contact by email and telephone

If you contact us by email or telephone, we may process your name, contact details, email address or telephone number, the time of contact, the content of your message and information you provide voluntarily.

Purposes: Data are processed to handle and answer the enquiry, conduct business correspondence, enter into or perform a contract and maintain necessary records.

Legal bases: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where the communication concerns entering into or performing a contract. General business enquiries are processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the appropriate handling, documentation and answering of business enquiries. Article 6(1)(c) GDPR applies where legal retention or documentation obligations exist.

Recipients or categories of recipients: Data may be received by the responsible persons within the company and by service providers where this is necessary for communication. These include providers of email, telecommunications and information technology services.

Retention periods and deletion criteria

Data from contact enquiries are deleted once the enquiry has been fully dealt with and no further reason for retention exists. Further retention may be necessary for performing a contract, complying with a statutory retention obligation or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Where statutory retention obligations apply, the relevant data are retained until the applicable period expires and then deleted.

Technical data for which GitHub is responsible are subject to GitHub’s information on retention and deletion.

Voluntary provision of data

Providing personal data when making a general enquiry is normally neither a statutory nor a contractual requirement. However, an enquiry may not be handled or answered without the contact details and information needed for a response.

Where the communication relates to entering into or performing a contract, certain information may be necessary for that purpose.
