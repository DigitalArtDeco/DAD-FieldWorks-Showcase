# Quantum Hardware Direction

DAD FieldWorks is being developed toward validation-aware RF and microwave workflows that may support future quantum-hardware-oriented engineering context.

Current public material focuses on electromagnetic diagnostics, RF PCB workflows, microwave structures, cavity and resonator analysis, and reproducible evidence records. It does not claim a quantum device simulator.

## RF And Microwave Context

The quantum hardware orientation is expressed through classical electromagnetic structures such as resonators, cavities, transmission-line diagnostics, field-mode analysis and resonator ringdown diagnostics. These structures can matter for device environments, packaging modes and coupling regions, but the public material does not model qubit states, Josephson junctions, Hamiltonians, decoherence or cryogenic device behavior.

## Evidence-Controlled Direction

The core idea is that every computed result should carry its evidence level, limitations and claim boundary. This applies to RF PCB workflows, microwave resonator diagnostics and any future quantum-hardware-oriented workflow.

A future DGTD route, PINN route or neural-operator route would be used only as a candidate field or mode generator until its output passes residual, boundary-condition, finite-value, reference and claim-boundary gates.
