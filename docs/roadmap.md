# Public Roadmap

The public roadmap is limited to documentation, showcase presentation, and public-safe descriptions of validation-aware RF and quantum hardware design workflow direction.

Potential public additions may include:

- clearer evidence contract examples
- updated public companion notes for claim boundaries
- additional public-safe generated diagrams
- quantum-hardware-oriented resonator and cavity workflow notes
- RF PCB, cavity, and resonator evidence-chain notes
- future whitepaper revisions from a separate public source workflow

Any future material should preserve the distinction between showcase communication and validation evidence.

Current public priorities:

- keep legal publication readiness explicit
- keep generated assets license safe
- keep private implementation details out of the public site
- keep validation, production, and equivalence claims blocked unless supported by a separate public evidence record
- keep quantum-hardware-oriented wording tied to RF and microwave diagnostics until stronger evidence exists
- describe records-only PEC cavity residual, generalized mass, and divergence or gauge closure planning without implying solver readiness
- defer GUI and production-core claims until core workflows and claim boundaries mature
