# Microstrip Quasi TEM Provenance

The microstrip quasi TEM cross-section animation is generated from numerical field data.

- Solver or model family: finite-difference quasi-static cross-section solve.
- Geometry summary: top conductor trace above a dielectric substrate with a lower ground plane and air region above the substrate.
- Boundary conditions: top trace held at 1 V, lower ground plane held at 0 V, and conservative open side and top boundaries for the public diagnostic.
- Dielectric model: piecewise constant relative permittivity, with a higher-permittivity substrate and air above the substrate.
- Rendered quantities: electric potential, electric field lines computed from `E = -grad(V)`, and electric-field energy density proportional to `epsilon |E|^2`.
- Claim boundary: diagnostic visualization only, not validation evidence, not production readiness, and not a full wave solver claim.
- External source images: none.
- Screenshots: none.
- AI image generation: none.
- Private source code published: no.
