# Product communication review

Reviewed on 6 September 2026.

## Editorial purpose

The homepage addresses RF, microwave, signal integrity and PCB engineers investigating supported structures. It explains the engineering questions before the project workflow and technical documentation.

Previous headline: "From PCB geometry to fields and S parameters."

Current headline: "Understand reflection, transmission and field behavior."

The introduction identifies a Windows application, its audience, the available result types and the benefit of keeping model and results in one project. The development notice remains visible near the introduction.

## Page structure and 30-second review

The order is introduction, engineering questions, model-to-result workflow, result views, editable examples and materials, saved-result handling, development status and contact.

A reader of the introduction and first following section can answer:

- What is it? A Windows application for electromagnetic investigation of supported PCB and RF structures.
- Who is it for? RF and PCB engineers, with microwave and signal integrity work named in the next section.
- What can I investigate? Reflection and transmission, frequency-dependent input matching, geometry changes and field concentration.
- What results can I inspect? S parameters and saved electric or magnetic field components.
- Is production use permitted? No. It is a development preview; external validation is not yet complete.

This editorial review passed. It is a copy check, not a usability study or scientific validation.
