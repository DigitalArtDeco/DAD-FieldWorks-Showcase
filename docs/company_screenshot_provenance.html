Field interpretation

The three field images show the same saved step 4096, time 1.023875e-09 s. They are different slices or display modes, not a time sequence. H_y is a signed magnetic component in A/m, not the total field magnitude, magnetic flux density, power density or field lines.

Transparent surfaces and section contours locate the field within the authoring model. They do not show compiled cells or a newly generated mesh. This is a shielded TEM reference line, not an open microstrip or antenna example.

Smith replacement

The active Smith detail page now shows the Uniform Shielded TEM Reference Line, S(2,2), marker 5.500 GHz and a 50 ohm reference. The selected option is Smith: wavelength scales. The outer generator and load scales are graphical aids under the shown lossless-line convention, not a separate physical line-length or deembedding calculation.

This replaces the earlier active Via Transition Smith screenshot at 2.800 GHz. Its original image, manifest and dated provenance remain unchanged.

The Smith marker does not specify the frequency of the saved time-domain field slices. The retained Via Transition Cartesian view has its own project and frequency samples.
