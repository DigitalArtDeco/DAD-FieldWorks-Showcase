# PCB Trace Current And Field Animation

This animation shows a side view microstrip-like PCB visualization. The upper conductor trace, dielectric substrate, lower ground plane, current on the trace, field lines between trace and ground, and energy flow in the substrate are shown together.

- Rendered quantities: top-trace current magnitude, cross-sectional electric field lines, and substrate energy flow.
- Current coloring: red is high current magnitude and blue is low current magnitude.
- Modeling family: quasi-TEM microstrip transmission-line time-domain update with cross-sectional field rendering.
- Geometry: top conductor trace over a dielectric substrate and lower ground plane.
- Excitation: smooth voltage rise from near 0 V toward about 1 V at the input end.
- Boundary handling: matched outgoing line approximation at the far end.
- Creation method: solver generated numerical PCB visualization.

- Screenshots: none.
- AI image generation: none.
- Private solver source code published: no.
