Uniform TEM source · Internal numerical check

Comparing a pulse with a numerical reference

The upper panels show the electric field component Ez at one sampling location, first during the pulse and then over the complete saved interval. Blue identifies the 3D calculation and orange the separately implemented scalar reference. The lower panels show area-weighted electric and magnetic error norms at five cross-sections along the line, in V and A respectively. The two numerical implementations use the same defined test conditions; neither is an experimental measurement.

The source check concerns a uniform, homogeneous, lossless TEM line with ideal conducting boundaries. The reference uses a separate incident recurrence and the discrete axial operator. This is not a general error bound, a convergence study or a qualification of a coupled multiport device. All axes, original error curves and development notices are retained.

These examples document selected internal numerical checks under defined test conditions. They do not represent external certification or a general accuracy guarantee. Image provenance.
