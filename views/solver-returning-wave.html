Uniform TEM source · Internal numerical check

Checking returning waves

A denotes the incident excitation, R the returning excitation and AR a third calculation with both excitations active. In each direction, the upper panel compares R with AR minus A at the source location. The lower panel shows the raw difference AR minus A minus R over the complete saved interval. The returning pulse crosses the source region while the incident input remains active.

Development preview. External validation is not yet complete. Not released for production use.

These are separate calculations for the same uniform TEM source test family as the pulse-reference image, not independent experimental studies. The small superposition differences retain their original units and scales. The result applies only to the selected source, pulse parameters, basis, grid and time window; it does not establish general mode purity or qualify a multiport response.

These examples document selected internal numerical checks under defined test conditions. They do not represent external certification or a general accuracy guarantee. Image provenance.
